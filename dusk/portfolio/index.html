07

A 25 kHz centre-aligned PWM gives the current loop a 40 µs window

Fig A1.1 — System and current loop · drawn from the Rev 01 schematic

Each PWM period the G431 samples the three low-side shunts, runs Clarke and Park, two PI current loops and SVPWM, then updates the timer. The DRV8323S drives six 100 V FETs and amplifies the shunts; the AS5047P reports rotor angle over SPI.

A suspected shoot-through killed four servos, and the board had no way to tell us why

Fig B4.1 — After the shoot-through · Fig B4.2 — Exposure to the elements

Both switches of the buck half-bridge on at once shorts the input to ground. Nothing on the board could have caught it or recorded it. My own review of the season: "Dismissed a member's concern about a critical failure."
